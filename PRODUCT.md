# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Engineering and operations leaders running production AI agents (support resolution, claims, document pipelines) whose per-outcome cost is material and unmeasured. They arrive skeptical of AI-consulting theater; they buy evidence. Secondary reader: the finance owner who approves the $15k sprint.

## Product Purpose

Landing page for the Production AI Optimization Sprint — $15,000, 21 days, one production workflow, one shipped improvement graded against a preregistered baseline. Success: a qualified visitor emails Paul with the subject line. The page is also proof-of-craft: Slancha optimizes production systems, so its own site must read as an instrument, not a brochure.

## Brand Personality

Instrument-maker. Evidence-first. Austere confidence. The visual metaphor is a precision measuring device (the loop: measure → change → prove → deploy → grade → repeat). Motion is calibrated and physical, never decorative confetti.

## Anti-references

Generic AI-SaaS template: purple-blue gradients, glassmorphism cards, rounded-2xl grids, hero-metric blocks, emoji, stock rocket icons, testimonial walls. No invented numbers, logos, or customer results — the copy ledger (CLAIMS discipline) forbids them.

## Design Principles

- Instrument, not brochure: every visual should look measured or measurable.
- The loop is the product: the six-station loop is the one diagram; everything else is typography.
- Evidence over adjectives: copy states what is delivered and graded; the no-change result stays visible.
- Waymark palette only: #14100c ground, #ece7de ink, #c8a24a bronze, #c2543a sparingly for the warning register.
- One page, one action: every path ends at the contact email.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on all text. Full `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative (static frame, no rotor, no parallax). Keyboard-reachable anchors and visible focus. WebGL failure must degrade to a legible static page.
