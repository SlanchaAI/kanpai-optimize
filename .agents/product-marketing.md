# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-08-02

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** A 21-day, $15,000 fixed-price sprint that lowers what one verified outcome costs in a production AI workflow — with the evidence preregistered before the clock starts.
**What it does:** Slancha reads the traces a company already emits, joins spend to a verified result, preregisters a baseline + quality floor, ships one reversible improvement, and grades itself on a scorecard after the measurement window.
**Product category:** AI cost optimization services (buyers search: "reduce AI costs," "LLM cost optimization," "inference cost," "AI FinOps").
**Product type:** Productized consulting sprint.
**Business model:** $15,000 fixed fee per sprint, one workflow. Fee buys the measurement; the shipped change is the upside.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Companies spending $25,000+/month on production AI (agents, models, tools) — LLM or non-LLM (speech, vision, video, document models).
**Decision-makers:** Founder/CTO at startups; VP Eng / head of platform at mid-size; whoever owns the AI bill.
**Primary use case:** The AI bill grew faster than anyone's ability to explain it; every team pulls on the workflow, no one owns its unit economics.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Make my cost-per-outcome number defensible to my CFO/board.
- Cut the bill without quietly degrading quality.
- Find out which of ~10 optimization levers actually applies to us.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Production AI spend is unattributed and rising; savings claims from vendors are unverifiable.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Platforms ("50-90% cheaper") require migrating onto them — the discount's price is lock-in.
- Big firms sell $500k transformations; "outcome-based" means a privately negotiated business case.
- FinOps/observability tools measure and alert; nobody ships the intervention.
**What it costs them:** Real dollars monthly, plus decision paralysis — every optimization claim in the market is seller-graded.
**Emotional tension:** Suspicion (every vendor claims 10x), fear of breaking production quality to save money.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** AIDOLS-style guarantee consultancies — vague "efficiency" promises, no measurement instrument.
**Secondary:** Baseten FDEs / platform experts — free, but condition = run on our platform. Helicone/Braintrust gateways — automate routing/caching only.
**Indirect:** Do nothing / internal eng time — no baseline, savings never proven.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Price on the page ($15k; nobody else publishes one).
- Preregistered evidence contract — baseline, quality floor, measurement window locked before work starts (nobody else does this).
- Platform-neutral: runs on the client's stack, reads traces they already emit.
- Covers the models nobody watches (speech, vision, video) — not just LLMs.
**Why customers choose us:** The number survives their CFO.
