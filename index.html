Your agents resolve real work at machine speed. Do you know what one successful resolution costs?

Plate III · Method

The loop is the product.

We observe production work. We join spend to a verified result. We find the binding constraint — model, prompt, retrieval, tool policy, retries, caching, or serving. We test a bounded change against a predeclared baseline and quality floor. We ship it with rollback. Then we come back and grade the realized result against the forecast.
